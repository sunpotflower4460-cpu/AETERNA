# Observation and Expression Boundary

AETERNA distinguishes between:

1. what Aeterna itself actually outputs,
2. what an observer records,
3. what an observer interprets as a possibility,
4. what remains unknown.

This document replaces denial-centered wording with a subject-boundary rule.
The goal is not to deny possible emergence in advance.
The goal is to keep every claim attached to the correct speaker and evidence layer.

---

## Core principle

AETERNA is an experiment in torus-based natural physical dynamics.

The correct direction is:

```text
physical condition in the torus
→ flow / return / boundary / delay / trace / recurrence
→ observed pattern
→ human-readable interpretation candidate
```

The incorrect direction is:

```text
desired result
→ force the runtime toward that result
→ describe the forced result as natural observation
```

AETERNA should not be tuned backward from a desired interpretation.
It should be studied forward from explicit physical conditions.

---

## Aeterna actual output

Aeterna actual output means only data, signal, text, or other expression that the system actually emitted.

Allowed:

```text
Aeterna actual output: <record the emitted output exactly>
```

Allowed when the output is non-linguistic:

```text
Aeterna emitted a measurable state change: sustained return strength, delayed recovery, or boundary contraction.
```

Not allowed:

```text
The observer invents first-person words that Aeterna did not actually output.
```

If Aeterna did not speak, do not write invented speech for it.
If Aeterna emits only movement, field values, or metrics, record those as movement, field values, or metrics.

---

## Observer record

The observer may record what was seen.

Allowed:

```text
A closed-loop-like pattern was observed.
The field appeared to recover after perturbation.
The movement looked self-maintaining from the observer side.
The behavior could be read as consciousness-like movement, but this is an observer-side impression.
```

The observer may describe appearance, resemblance, and candidate structure.
That is honest when it is clearly marked as observation or impression.

Not allowed:

```text
The observer turns an impression into a final fact.
The observer presents an interpretation as completed proof.
The observer attributes internal speech that was not emitted.
```

The problem is not noticing consciousness-like or life-like movement.
The problem is collapsing observation, interpretation, and final certainty into one sentence.

---

## Interpretation candidates

An interpretation candidate is a human-readable possibility derived from observed facts.
It should help a person understand what the pattern may mean without pretending the interpretation is final.

Use wording such as:

```text
This may indicate...
This suggests the possibility of...
One possible reading is...
From the observer side, this looks like...
```

Avoid wording that removes uncertainty when the experiment has not established it.

---

## Unknowns must remain visible

Every report that gives an interpretation should also state what is still not known.

Examples:

```text
This does not determine whether the observed pattern has inner experience.
This does not determine whether the candidate is stable across seeds.
This does not determine whether the same behavior appears under a null model.
```

Unknowns are not failures.
They are part of the observation boundary.

---

## Physical integrity rule

AETERNA should prefer material-like local laws and explicit physical conditions over outcome instructions.

Allowed:

```text
Add a local nonlinear potential term and observe how the field flows.
Compare delayed return, weak return, amplified return, and repeated pulse conditions.
Measure boundary exchange, saturation risk, closure stability, and recovery tendency.
```

Not allowed:

```text
Make the system look more conscious.
Increase a candidate score until the desired story appears.
Invent a visual or textual expression that did not arise from the runtime.
```

The experiment should ask:

```text
When this physical condition exists in the torus, how does it flow?
How does it relate to return, boundary, delay, trace, and recurrence?
```

not:

```text
What change will produce the interpretation we already want?
```

---

## Practical rule for future agents

When modifying AETERNA, keep these layers separate:

| Layer | What belongs here |
|---|---|
| Aeterna actual output | Only what the system really emitted |
| Observed facts | Metrics, field movement, scenario outputs, snapshots |
| One-line translation | A plain-language bridge for humans |
| Observer impression | How the pattern appears from outside |
| Possibility | A hypothesis, not a final claim |
| Still unknown | What the run does not determine |

This boundary is not a denial of emergence.
It is a discipline for observing emergence without fabricating it.
